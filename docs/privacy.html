Probe Dash privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

In short

Probe Dash has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location. Your game progress stays on your phone. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google collects some data from your device to do that.

What stays on your phone

Your crystals, upgrades, best distance, run count and settings are saved in a file on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Ads and what Google collects

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the game collects your device's advertising ID, IP address (which shows your approximate region), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google acts on this data under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before loading ads, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings > Privacy choices. On any Android phone you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the phone's Settings > Privacy > Ads. Watching an ad for a reward is always optional.

Children

Probe Dash is not directed at children under 13, and ads are limited to content rated for teens or younger audiences.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, we do not run our own analytics in this version, and we do not receive the data Google collects in a form that identifies you.

Changes

If a later version collects more (for example anonymous play statistics), this policy will be updated first and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at https://github.com/SouthIcarus/probe-dash/issues or use the developer contact shown on the game's Google Play page.
